## Wall and governance unit

Implement text wall/help posts and flat replies, owner edit/resolve/close/reopen/delete, public/member visibility, own published feed and private inbox references. No image upload until real storage/deletion exists. Extend notifications with post references; deleted/hidden/blocked content has no preview.

Persistent bilateral signed-in blocks hide authored posts/activities/replies, prevent new interaction and suppress new reply notices. Public content can still be read as a visitor; a block is not a confidentiality boundary for public publication. Blocking an organizer/participant pair withdraws their active participation and clears saved calendar/preference; unblocking does not rejoin. Blocking other attendees does not erase shared activity history or pretend they cannot be present physically.

Reports target visible post/reply/activity/activity-comment, private to reporter and admins. No copied content snapshot; deleted content is unavailable to review. Admin-only queue and versioned resolve actions (dismiss, hide content, ban author), with audit reason and read suppression/cancel/withdraw consequences. Protect admin accounts from report-based bans. This unit adds API enforcement; restricted web administration remains E5.

Validation uses temporary databases/accounts: ownership, visibility, reply idempotency, solved state, deletion/export, bilateral filtering and notification previews, block-related withdrawal/promotion, report privacy/duplicate retry, nonadmin denial, actual hide/ban effects and deleted-target handling. Add native wall and report/block controls; do not claim simulator acceptance from bundle compilation.
