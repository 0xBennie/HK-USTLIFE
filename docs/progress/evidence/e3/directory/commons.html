Information Commons & Learning Commons
Information Commons - IC

Technology Items for Loan

The Information Commons (G/F) and Learning Commons (LG1) are supportive learning spaces that provide a variety of computers, equipment, group study rooms and services.

Information Commons – IC

At the IC, you can find computer workstations for individual work as well as small-group discussion. Printing and scanning machines are close by. The IC Learning Space with a 136-inch LED wall display is suitable for presentations or small classes.

Computer Workstations

Learning Commons – LC

The LC opens 24-hours during Fall terms and Spring terms. It consists of different components. To book a study room, use the online booking system; to run a teaching session, set up tutorials, or other academic activities at the LC, check the teaching venues option or talk with us directly.

18 Group Study Rooms
Open Study Area
Refreshment Zone

The space for taking a break from studying – have a light snack and refreshment, watch TV, or just hang around.
E-Learning Classroom A, B
Tutorial Spaces

Creative Media Zone

The Creative Media Zone supports graphics and media production for learning and teaching. It consists of the Media Production Studio (with a control room and a dressing room), 4 AV Editing Suites, and the Graphics Workshop. The facilities and services are operated by Media Technology and Publishing Center, which has a service counter inside the Graphics Workshop.

View Website
